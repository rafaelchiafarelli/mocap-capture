# bugs — mocap-capture

A backlog, not an initiative that finishes. Each bug is recorded here and
fixed on a `fixes/<id>/<name>` branch when it gets in the way (the
`mocap-workflow` skill, "Bugs").

Epics by area (last number used):

- `environment` — the machine the recorder runs on (1)
- `preprocess` — what the recorder does to the video after a take (2)
