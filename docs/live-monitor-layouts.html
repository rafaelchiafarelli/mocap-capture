Director monitor layouts

The live cameras on the recorder PC's screen. All four layouts are available and the operator switches between them at any time; the default comes from config.yaml. Feeds are the tablets' portrait 1200 × 1600 images on a 16:9 display. Body cameras sit one per side of the capture cube; the face camera is likely, the hand cameras are TBD. Click any feed to make it the selected camera.
